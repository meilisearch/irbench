Scenario id: `knowledge-base/web-questions`. It tests question answering over
plain text: real web-search questions against answers from cooking, travel and
sports forums.

## Source

- **Dataset:** LoTTE lifestyle, search queries, from the Hugging Face mirrors
  `colbertv2/lotte_passages` and `colbertv2/lotte`, pinned to a commit.
  Queries come from Google, via GooAQ.
- **License:** Apache-2.0 on the mirror's card; the underlying content is
  StackExchange, CC BY-SA.
- **Size:** 119,461 documents, 661 queries, 1,804 judgments.

## Fields and modes

| Role | Fields |
|---|---|
| Searchable | `text` |
| Filterable, sortable, facets | none |

Modes: `keyword`, `semantic`, `hybrid`. The documents have no metadata, so
there are no `filtered`, `sorted` or `faceted` modes. Embeddings use `text`.

## Queries and judgments

Queries are real Google questions. The judgments are the accepted answers of
the matching forum question: usually one or two documents per query, so they
are sparse. Many correct answers are not judged, which lowers every absolute
score; compare engines to each other, not to other benchmarks. Scores are
reported per forum (cooking, travel, sports) next to the overall scores.

## What was dropped

Nothing: no document and no query was dropped. See
[Methodology](/methodology/overview#dataset-and-qrels-grades).

## Perturbations

`typo` and `prefix`, seed `20261014`. There is no synonym perturbation and no
synonym list.

## Tuned track

Meilisearch's `tuned` track sets `matchingStrategy: frequency`, carrying the
WANDS finding over to question-style queries, where the default `last` drops
the final words of a question first.
