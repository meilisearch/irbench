title: "Scenario: covid-papers"
description: Document search over 171k CORD-19 papers with graded human judgments (TREC-COVID)
---

Scenario id: `knowledge-base/covid-papers`. It tests document search: long
abstracts, graded judgments, and real metadata (date, source) to filter, sort
and facet on.

## Source

- **Dataset:** TREC-COVID, from the BEIR archive, joined with the CORD-19
  `metadata.csv` of 2020-07-16 on `cord_uid`.
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (BEIR). CORD-19 papers carry their own licenses,
  listed in `metadata.csv`.
- **Size:** 171,331 documents, 100 queries, 132,672 judgments (grades 0, 1, 2; the 66,336 judgments of the 50 topics, once per query form).

## Fields and modes

| Role | Fields |
|---|---|
| Searchable (priority order) | `title`, `text` (the abstract) |
| Filterable | `source`, `publish_year` |
| Sortable | `publish_date` (`YYYYMMDD`) |
| Facets | `source` |

Modes: `keyword`, `semantic`, `hybrid`, `filtered`, `sorted`, `faceted`.
`filtered`, `sorted` and `faceted` run on `keyword` and `semantic`.
Filter templates: `source` equals a value, `publish_year` at least 2020. Sort
template: `publish_date` descending. Embeddings use `title` and `text`.

## Queries

Each of the 50 TREC-COVID topics gives two queries that share the topic's
judgments:

- **question:** the natural-language question ("what is the origin of COVID-19");
- **keyword:** the short keyword form ("coronavirus origin").

Scores are reported per form, next to the overall scores. With 100 queries
(50 per form), confidence intervals are wide: read small differences between
engines as ties.

## What was dropped

One document with no title and no abstract, and no judgments with it. The
grades of -1 in the source judgments become 0. No query was dropped. See
[Methodology](/methodology/overview#dataset-and-qrels-grades).

## Perturbations

`typo` and `prefix`, seed `20261014`. There is no synonym perturbation and no
synonym list: no domain vocabulary was built for this scenario.

## Tuned track

Meilisearch's `tuned` track sets `matchingStrategy: frequency`, carrying the
WANDS finding over to this scenario. Its default config moves `sort` first in
the ranking rules, so that `publish_date` sorting is strict.
